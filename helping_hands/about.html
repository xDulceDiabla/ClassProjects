About Helping Hands

Helping Hands of Whitfield Co. Inc. is a family owned and operated cleaning business proudly serving North Georgia and the Chattanooga area for more than 15 years. We are committed to providing dependable service, attention to detail, and results you can truly see and feel.

💙 Our Story 💙

Helping Hands of Whitfield Co. Inc. is proudly owned and operated by husband and wife team, Kate and Dallas Nelson. What started as a simple idea quickly grew into a business built on hard work, dedication, and a genuine passion for helping others. Kate and Dallas have worked side by side to build a company known for dependable service, attention to detail, and care you can truly feel.

Every space they clean is treated with the same level of pride and respect as their own home. They understand that a clean space is more than just appearance. It’s about creating an environment that feels fresh, welcoming, and stress-free for every client.

From offices and day care centers to medical facilities and specialty floor care, their team is committed to delivering consistent, high quality results you can rely on. Their goal has always been simple: to leave every space better than they found it and every customer feeling valued. When you choose Helping Hands, you’re not just hiring a cleaning service you’re supporting a family-run business that truly cares about your space and your experience.
